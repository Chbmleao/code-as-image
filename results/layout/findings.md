# Leading indentation in the packed layout (UFM-30)

Exploratory, outside methods 3.3. Generated by `scripts/layout_share.py` from `layout.json`; no model was run. Every page of the main run was rendered again and matched it (9722 pages, 995 items).

Share: leading spaces over all rendered characters (the newline marker counts as one), per item; the same at every size, since lines flow across rows. Area saved: 1 minus the page area without leading indentation over the page area now, per item. Quantiles are type 1.

## CoderUJB (136 items)

Share: mean 17.3% (SD 4.6%), median 16.3% (IQR 14.1% to 19.6%), pooled 17.7%.

| Size | Pages | Pages without indentation | Area saved, median (IQR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 px | 286 | 244 | 16.4% (14.2% to 19.6%) |
| 12 px | 399 | 329 | 16.2% (14.1% to 19.7%) |
| 14 px | 520 | 448 | 16.3% (14.1% to 19.6%) |

## DevEval (859 items)

Share: mean 18.4% (SD 4.2%), median 18.3% (IQR 15.7% to 20.6%), pooled 18.6%.

| Size | Pages | Pages without indentation | Area saved, median (IQR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 px | 2224 | 2020 | 18.1% (15.6% to 20.2%) |
| 12 px | 2783 | 2444 | 18.2% (15.6% to 20.4%) |
| 14 px | 3510 | 3004 | 18.3% (15.6% to 20.4%) |

**Verdict: small.** The threshold, fixed before the numbers (docs/decisions.md, 2026-11-17), is a median area saving below 27.3% in every benchmark and size: the smallest step of the font sweep in the area a character costs.

## Paragraph for the limitations

One renderer was used. It packs the code: lines run on across each row with a visible newline marker, so the blank space at line ends is not in our images, but leading indentation is kept so that the code's structure survives. CodeShrink (Tang et al., 2026) reports that rendering code without blank space raises Qwen3-VL completion at 1x from 36.2 to 54.7 edit similarity (against a baseline whose compression ratio we could not confirm), so part of an image penalty can come from the layout rather than the modality. Leading spaces make up 17.3% on CoderUJB and 18.4% on DevEval of our rendered characters (item means). Removing them would cut the median item's page area by 16.2% to 16.4% on CoderUJB and 18.1% to 18.3% on DevEval, depending on the font size. For scale, the smallest step of our font sweep changes the area a character costs by 27.3%, and in that sweep larger fonts raised the pass rate of some models, so area freed from indentation and spent on a larger font could matter. We did not render the contexts without indentation or run any model on such images, so a layout effect is not ruled out and we make no claim about its size for our models.

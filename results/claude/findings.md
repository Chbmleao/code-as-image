# Claude extension findings

Generated by `uv run python scripts/analysis.py report --models all` from results/claude/analysis/{gaps,replies,ranks,glmm,contamination}.json, glmm_contrast_cov.csv and results/analysis/{gaps,glmm}.json; edit the scripts, not this file.

Every number here is exploratory (methodology addendum, 2026-11-14). Models: Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Opus 5.5, runs 0 and 1 on every item. Gap = image minus text, mean over the image sizes, in percentage points. Intervals: CR1 cluster t with G - 1 df; item line: paired t. Holm over the 4 Claude size-averaged gaps; one BH set over the per-size gaps, the size differences and the gaps without flagged items (40 rows). The logit contrast is biased toward 0 by 0.03 to 0.13 logit in the UFM-21 simulation.

Run differences from the open models (addendum):

- No seed is sent, since the API takes none.
- No temperature or top_p is sent, so both models sample at temperature 1.0.
- Opus cannot turn thinking off and runs adaptive thinking at low effort.
- An Opus reply over 2,048 non-thinking tokens fails before grading (`over_budget`).
- 2 runs on every item, where the open models have 3 on block-1 items.
- Closed weights and training data: the copy flags are the only contamination check.

Analysis deviations:

- The 7-model line drops an item with a short Claude cell in all four conditions (decisions 2026-11-17).
- A short Claude cell stops `gaps --models claude` instead of leaving its condition; the collected tables have none.
- The token ratio is the median per-item ratio, as for the open models; the item mean that the addendum names is shown beside it.
- Claude output tokens leave thinking out.

## Paper paragraph

In an exploratory extension under a methodology addendum written before the first request, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Opus 5.5 answered the same items in the same four conditions, two runs each. They differ from the open-model run in three ways: the API takes no seed, it samples at temperature 1.0, and Opus cannot turn thinking off. Their text pass rates were CoderUJB 97.4% and 97.4%; DevEval 86.7% and 94.2%. Averaged over the three image sizes, the image-minus-text gaps (95% cluster intervals) were CoderUJB -6.2 pp [-10.8, -1.7] and -2.0 pp [-3.7, -0.3]; DevEval -6.1 pp [-8.1, -4.2] and -1.8 pp [-3.2, -0.3]; all 4 cluster intervals exclude 0. The item-level interval includes 0 for Claude Opus 5.5 on CoderUJB [-4.3, +0.4]. The open models' gaps ranged over CoderUJB -36.2 to -12.8 pp; DevEval -31.5 to -16.5 pp. The open vs closed logit contrast c (Claude mean minus open mean, 95% Wald interval) was CoderUJB 2.75 [1.40, 4.10], Claude loses less (interval above 0); DevEval 3.80 [3.33, 4.26], Claude loses less (interval above 0). With seven models the Wald test of equal contrasts on 6 df gave CoderUJB chi2 45.5, p 3.8e-08; DevEval chi2 338.7, p 4.14e-70. On CoderUJB, 97.1% and 95.6% of items pass both Claude text runs, and another model's text answer reproduces the reference token for token on 126 and 106 of its 136 items.
